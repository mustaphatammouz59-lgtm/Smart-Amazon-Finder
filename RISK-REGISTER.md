# Risk Register — Smart Amazon Finder

Stage 00. Status: living document, created 2026-11-08. Likelihood/Impact: Low / Medium / High. Every risk has an owner (role) and a trigger that forces re-review.

| ID | Risk | L | I | Mitigation (designed) | Trigger / early warning | Owner |

| R-19 | **Fake-data temptation under deadline pressure** (inventing a price or rating "temporarily"). | Low | High | Schema + render tests make it technically hard; trust rules in PRD §7 are acceptance criteria, not guidelines. | Any request to "just put a number". | PM |
| R-20 | **Email deliverability / account-system abuse** if user accounts launch (spam signups, reset abuse). | Medium | Low | Rate limits, verification before privileges, challenge on signup if abused; accounts are optional to the product. | Signup anomaly metrics. | Security |

## Review cadence
Register reviewed at every stage gate; likelihoods re-scored with evidence; closed risks are marked closed with the date and evidence, never deleted.
